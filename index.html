    About

    Hi, I’m Aryan. This is my little corner of the internet for the things I’m figuring out and working on.

    Coding doesn’t come naturally to me (yet), so I’m not the fastest coder in the room—and that’s fine. I’d rather understand why something works than copy-paste and hope for the best. Away from a screen, that same curiosity usually ends up under a car’s bonnet.

    Whether it’s how an engine turns fuel into motion or why a model gives one answer instead of another, I like looking beneath the surface. Two very different machines; one recurring question: what’s actually going on? Explaining it without sounding like a textbook is a nice bonus.
